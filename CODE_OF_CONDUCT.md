# Code of conduct

## Our standard

AMARACUS's issues, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces are for working on the software.
Everyone taking part is expected to:

- be respectful and professional, including when disagreeing;
- criticise ideas and code, not people;
- accept that maintainers make the final decision on what is merged;
- respect people's privacy and never publish someone else's personal information;
- report security problems privately (see SECURITY.md).

Not acceptable: harassment, threats, insults, discriminatory language or imagery about anyone's age, body,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality, race, religion or sexual orientation;
sexualised content; trolling; spam; or encouraging any of these.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may warn, temporarily block or permanently ban anyone who
does not follow this code, in proportion to what happened.

Report problems to **conduct@amaracus.tech**. Reports are handled confidentially, and nobody is penalised for
a report made in good faith.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.
