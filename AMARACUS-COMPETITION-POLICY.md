# AMARACUS competition policy

This page explains how AMARACUS reads the **Noncompete** section of the PolyForm Shield License 1.0.0
(`LICENSE`), which applies to every copy, modification and fork of AMARACUS. It does not add restrictions:
where this page and the licence differ, the licence governs. Section 10 of the
[AMARACUS Additional Commercial Terms](AMARACUS-ADDITIONAL-COMMERCIAL-TERMS.md) points here.

## The rule

You may use AMARACUS for any purpose **except providing a product that competes with AMARACUS**, or with any
product AMARACUS or its affiliates provide using AMARACUS. Shield adds that:

- products compete even through different interfaces, platforms or programming languages;
- products compete even when they are free;
- a product marketed as a practical substitute for AMARACUS definitely competes.

## Not allowed (examples)

- Rebranding an AMARACUS fork and selling it as another cryptographic infrastructure platform.
- Adding one or two features to AMARACUS and selling or offering the result as a competing product.
- Building a competing key management service (KMS) with AMARACUS source code.
- Building a competing cryptographic control plane with AMARACUS source code.
- Rewriting AMARACUS in another programming language, or wrapping it in another interface (CLI, SDK, GUI,
  API, plugin), and offering that as a competing product.
- Offering a managed cryptographic API, encryption-as-a-service or key-management-as-a-service to third
  parties using AMARACUS code, whether paid or free.
- Offering a hosted "AMARACUS-compatible" service to the public using AMARACUS code.

## Allowed (examples)

- A bank running AMARACUS internally to protect its own data and keys.
- A company integrating AMARACUS into its own application, where the application's value is not
  cryptographic infrastructure offered to others (for example a hospital records system, a payments app or
  a SaaS product that encrypts its own customers' data with AMARACUS behind the scenes).
- A developer modifying AMARACUS for personal use, study or internal use.
- A business using AMARACUS as an internal security component of its own systems, including across all its
  subsidiaries.
- A consultant or integrator installing and operating AMARACUS for one client, on that client's
  infrastructure and under that client's own licence.
- Writing about, teaching, benchmarking or security-testing AMARACUS.

Every allowed use above is still subject to the lifetime allowance and payment terms.

## Independent development is free

Nothing stops anyone from building a competing product **without using or copying AMARACUS code**. Knowing
how AMARACUS works from its documentation or public behaviour is not prohibited; copying, translating or
deriving from its source code to build a competitor is.

## Unsure?

Write to legal@amaracus.tech before you build. A written answer from AMARACUS about your specific product
is binding on AMARACUS for that product.
